## Friday - September 18th, 2026 | "Can't Even Afford Internet"

It's been a while dear readers, who I know don't exist yet, but hopefully will soon. Two weeks since I last wrote anything on WeisGarden and it will be only hours before I finally share my digital garden with the world. There's a lot to catch up on so let me just get right to it.

Before I dive into the details, just want to take a moment and say thank you for reading this digital garden. I've been working on publishing it for quite some time now and it just hasn't happened for a few reasons, which aren't necesssarily important because what is important is that I get into the habit of writing and sharing my writing with the world.

So if you notice me slacking, definitely call me out on it. I've made my life much more difficult by not sharing my work and writing as much as I should. I also think I've got some good ideas to share and feel like there are some things I could teach or help people with.

Again, thank you for taking the time to read my newsletter, blog posts, digital garden, and especially thank you for anyone who has helped support me financially as money has been so, so, sooooo tight for about three years now and it helps me to be able to work on things like writing, coding, and working on my business instead of figuring out how to find enough money to eat everyday. [^1]

Again, thank you for your support as a reader or donor! Now, let me get back to recapping my last two weeks.

### No Internet = No New Content = No WeisGarden v1.0.0

I don't know if I've shared this yet, but I was homeless with my fiance Lisa for over three years, which was way too long. All I'll say on that is that it is very hard to get back on your feet if you go through something like what we went through, which we didn't really have a whole lot we could have done to avoid that scenario. It was just really bad...

...Anyways, we finally got the help we needed from the amazing non-profit, [Housing For New Hope](https://housingfornewhope.com) in Durham, North Carolina. They provide temporary housing for chronically homeless individuals and families [^2] in Durham and they got us into our current apartment back in July and it has been incredible for us. [^3]

If I'm being completely honest though, we are struggling financially. A lot of it comes from being dead broke when we were homeless, and I mean we had zero monies. I did work at Walmart for a summer, but they treated me like shit for being homeless so I quit - like everything else its a long story that I reference in the footnotes. [^4]

The cycle of poverty is vicious. Since we had no money moving into our apartment, we still had to do our street hustling stuff to survive - mainly, we had to panhandle. Ugh. 

Now, I will say that we did dumpster dive and sell stuff and I also picked up freelance software and web development projects from time to time when it was possible. But mainly panhandling was how we survived and it is not lucrative by any means. So it was disheartening that we have had to still do that to eat and afford things, which we actually really can't afford... especially when that thing is the internet.

If there are any developers out there reading this, imagine how hard it is to build a web application with no internet. That has been a frustrating problem for about three years now as I have been limited by the hours of Wegmans, Panera, and Starbucks. I still managed to learn a lot, accomplish freelance projects, and created my business/brands, which I am very proud of.

Well moving into the apartment, one of the first things I did was get internet because we don't have a mobile phone plan and I also haved needed to prepare for interviewing for jobs. This was all going swimmingly well and I was doing interviews, setting em up, and about to knock them down when the unthinkable happened - it became 50 days after the first due date of our Spectrum bill and they shut our internet off.

So I have had to try and figure out how to get $185 together through completing surveys, participating in sports betting, and trying to find gigs on Craigslist. It hurts because we have felt so close to being able to achieve our goals again and boom, no fucking internet. Sigh... but its ok, I am a positive and resilient person, so here I am.
