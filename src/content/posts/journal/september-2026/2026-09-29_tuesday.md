## Tuesday - September 29th, 2026 | "Workin on Creative Stuff and Writin Code"

One of the greatest parts of sitting down in front of the computer is it usually means I'm getting deep into code, writing, or coming up with ideas. There's some other stuff that goes on in there but I love nothing more than sitting and pondering how to address a problem or make an idea come to life with language that is processed by a machine in an efficient and algorithmic manner.

I guess this is a good promotion or introduction to kind of the main project I'm working on right now, [Geaux Computer Science](/src/content/notes/seedlings/geaux-computer-science/index.md), my guide to computer science under my brand Geaux Code. I am also working on a new website for this brand for sharing content.

Back to the process of creating software, it can be an excruciatingly difficult process, but it is ultimately a satisfying one. I will create a blog post or a brief guide on planning, designing, and creating software from scratch as it does require a lot of patience, creative thought, but also requires discipline.

Sometimes you will sit there for hours without anything seemingly getting done from the outside, but if you look at any of my notebooks or note tools, there is a lot going on the entire time.

Sometimes I am thinking and brain storming or problem solving, but most of the time I'm reading, learning, taking notes, diagramming, designing, and planning - believe it or not, the actual easy part for me now is writing the code.

There are different methodologies for writing software like Test Driven Development and Domain Driven Development, which are software architecture principles, but the same thing kind of goes for all software developers: dig in and get cozy cause you are going to be in front of that computer for quite some time - hell, I've spent *8-12 hours at my computer maybe getting up once or twice, and I know for sure there was a time I sat for 10 hours without standing up* because I was so wrapped up in my process of writing software, albeit doing it alone.

Here is the seedling for the [Geaux Code Software Design & Development Process Guide](/src/content/notes/seedlings/gc-software-design-and-dev-process.md/index.md) so you can follow along as I work on it and possibly support it if you want to help me out!
