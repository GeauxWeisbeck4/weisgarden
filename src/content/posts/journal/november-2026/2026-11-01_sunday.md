I've spent far too much time not logging my work in this project, which is a shame because the whole point of creating my digital garden was so I could share my knowledge, work, and content-in-the-works with those who may be interested. I have barely published many entries in my journal post section of this thing, as far as I can tell - which isn't necessarily important, but that would be the easiest way to add content in my opinion.

So it appears to me that I am going to need to make some changes this month if I want to meet my personal goals and dreams. I'm not going to make excuses, but it has been a rather challenging last few months, so I'm not going to be too hard on myself, but I am publicly admitting that I need to put more effort and time into my writing and developing, at least in public.

From here on out, I will try to add some type of content to this digital garden at least once each day, which isn't too outrageous - it doesn't even have to be a publicly published post to count, just a simple journal entry, note, or updates on work I've done on certain projects. For each piece of content I add, I will try to post a journal entry, even if it just includes what content was added that day or what I worked on, etc.

Doesn't seem too bad to me. This will help me get more content out there on the web, something I've really been bad at for quite a while now. If you read this, please hold me to it whatever ways you can, please! :)

Also, I am going to try and standardize my journal entries a little bit so I can create more of a workflow that makes this routine efficient and easy to complete.
