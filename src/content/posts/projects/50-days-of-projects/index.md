## 50 Days of Projects

Well, I have decided to take on an ambitious project as it would appear. For the purpose of growing my portfolio and having potential MVPs to start working on sharing, I have decided to create 50 different full stack applications on 50 different days.

Each day will consist of a project preparation white board + task board to plan out the entire project. After creating and testing the app, I will complete a write-up about the project and then possibly create a video to share my content with everyone. How exciting is that?

Each write up will be created and shared here on the digital garden first. I'm putting together a little website for hosting all of the projects together with their writeups and other content that is created about them/topics they are related to. I will likely continue development on some of these projects, so it will be cool to have a place where I can release new versions, write about ideas, etc.

## Goals

1. To get a deeper understanding of all the included underlying technologies and frameworks.
2. To get more experience developing projects from scratch and working from planning to development with an AI tool.
3. Encourage followers to support my work based on the quality of project and content.
