## Software Design By Example and the Architecture of Open Source Applications by Greg Wilson

If you haven't ever picked up any of these free software design and development resources, I can't recommend them enough. Here are some links you need to check out immediately:

- ***[Software Design By Example - JavaScript](https://third-bit.com/sdxjs/)***
- ***[Software Design by Example - Python](https://third-bit.com/sdxpy/)***
- ***[The Architecture of Open Source Applications Book Website](https://aosabook.org/en/)***
  - Contains the following books:
    - ***[The Architecture of Open Source Applications, Vol 1 by Amy Brown & Greg Wilson](https://aosabook.org/en/v1/intro1.html)***
    - ***[AOSA Vol 2 - Amy Brown & Greg Wilson](https://aosabook.org/en/v2/intro2.html)***
    - ***[The Performance of Open Source Applications, by Tavish Armstrong](https://aosabook.org/en/posa/introduction.html)***
    - ***[500 Lines or Less, by Tavish Armstrong](https://aosabook.org/en/500L/introduction.html)***
