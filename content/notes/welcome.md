Welcome to WeisGarden, my digital garden I created to share my work with the world. If you haven't ever heard of digital gardens, I suggest checking out the following resources:

Hopefully that can explain the idea behind what's going on here. I recently have to figure out how to showcase my work that I'm actively creating to get feedback, generate interest, and earn income from my work now that I am able to get back into being creative full time for the first time in a long while, which I will discuss plenty about that learning experience.

## Background Problems

1. Sharing content that isn't ready to be published for the purpose of getting feedback, updating interested parties, and earning support of people willing to help me out.
2. How do I encourage the very important process of writing and publishing more of my writing and becoming consistent in sharing my writing.
3. How can I show that I'm staying busy and workig on stuff and what I now in a creative or interesting way?

## WeisGarden Planning and Design

Obviously, we design things that meet some type of need or desire that offers some type of utility or value. Otherwise we wouldn't take the time to put the effort forth to design thngs that we like to use.

Planning and designing WeisGarden all have revolved around solving these base problems. This is how this project does that:

1. Offer different phases of content to show progress, get feedback, and shape the form of ideas and projects.
2. Showcase needs to complete some projects so interested parties can help get ideas developed quicker or just to help out for the effort put forth into something constructive.
3. Make myself accountable to public by having to keep up with expectations for sharing work on digital garden, which I will be posting at least a journal entry every day.
4. A fun project that I can mold and shape however I want using Astro and JavaScript.
