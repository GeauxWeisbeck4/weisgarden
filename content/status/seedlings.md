## Seedlings - "Earliest Stage of Content"

> "Just the same way we would as if we were growing anything in a Garden" - [1] Problem #1: How should WeisGarden treat ideas for projects, content, or anything that could potentially become something to share with others?

As WeisGarden is based on the digital garden concept, it should hopefully be understood that this is very much my public workspace for sharing ideas, content, and projects as I work on them. Make no doubt, I will make some cool shit to add to here over time, but I consider it to be my public canvas.

Recently overcoming some major challenges in my life has been eye opening and motivating. I have not had any public creative presence for quite sometime, and there's not a lot that I can do about that at this point other than figuring out how to do that consistently moving forward. The past is neither here nor there, it is behind and I don't look back and wonder if I should turn around anymore. 

There's not a good fucking thing back there you haven't made sure to bring a long, a short list, but anything not on it is a trap. An alligator waiting along the bank of a bayou swamp as a young child wades into the water chasing a rolling ball his friend threw over his head and into the stinky, scary swamp water that really smells on a hot and muggy August 28th afternoon at a moment where there have been virtually no clouds in the sky for the day... *sooo bad*... and then **SNAP!**, the alligator snatches a baby dear and swims away quickly just a few feet away from the boy, who doesn't notice and grabs the ball and gets back out tossing it back to his friend and running back towards him as the Alligator whips the baby dear back and forth, playing keep away from the other 'gators trying to get at any limbs or things of the sort that are franticly trying to escape for only a few moments longer. A bigger gator rips the deer in half and swims away as our initial friend gets away from the rest with his half as the scavengers part at the sight of the alpha "Daddy" giant that has been living in this specific swamp for the last epoch here in Louisiana since the dinosaurs were smattered in the gulf by an asteroid forever ago when he was born and survived the post-apocoloyptic ending to the giant rulers of the earth, really the only kind of place that is fucking such a mysterious and spooky place that would have a dinosaur age alligator still living in it's Bayous. Damn Louisiana is fucking crazy.

Wow, ok. That took a wild turn. But anyways back to the problem solving aspect of this project. I want to get back into creating shit now that my life has the necessary stability to be able to create awesome shit. I know a digital garden is the perfect solution to do just this since it is basically a public workspace that people can learn from and collaborate with as, some type of indie community I guess? I don't know.

But I do understand that making a project just for the sake of it is not going to bring in the resource I really need most - money. I need to share more work and code to show the professional world that I haven't been sitting idly by, but actively and constantly learning. I'm working on some stuff, but I want to people to see specifically what is going on each day as I seek work and/or building my business ideas into reality. Here are the questions I asked myself that are going to be pivotal in designing the workflow, UI, and overall application/website:

1. **How does WeisGarden help me get a job or earn me enough money to get by?**
   - Sharing work at all stages of being created allows for early and important feedback that will shape the idea, project, or content into it's polished form by using this information to guide design and architectural decisions along the way. It will help solve problems and fix things through open source and collaboration.
   - People may want to support my work if they can see the effort put into what I'm creating, if they find my content or ideas useful, or if what I create offers value... or really lots of different reasons possibly too. Providing a glimpse into various projects that people may think are worthy of backing or may even be like "Well hayy, at least he's trying and not just doing drugs on the streets panhandling so let's send him some money so he can afford those stupid tools he always cries about not being able to use that he says will make his life so much easier, then shit let's send him $100 every week because I want to encourage him.", or for any other reasons, then I need to have that early on. I really need to focus on figuring out finances, so send any ideas!
2. **How do Seedlings help me achieve my goal of sharing ideas?**
   - By having a content form for ideas, outlines, projects, content, or whatever it may become, it allows me to have a public canvas for creating and sharing with others. Build it and they will come.
