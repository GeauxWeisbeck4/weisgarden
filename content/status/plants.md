Plants are still farily high-level, but more detailed than a seedling. A draft perhaps is more of the idea here. It may also serve as a base idea/concept for many different branches of the initial seedling, now turned plant.

For example, my seedling idea to teach new developers Python and JavaScript is validated through research, writing, and feedback, so it moves into the plant phase. Initially the main idea is that I will start to teach others through blog posts and notes so I will do that on my digital garden. I also realize that I need to make a course because there are people telling me they will pay for one as soon as I create it. I would then create two branches for this plant content (an optional sort of diagonal direction phase, somewhat horizontal and mostly vertical), one for blog posts and one for the online course to have their own plant extension of the initial plant that remains connected, but obtains the same ideas.

The idea here is that once it is a plant you can be certain it is being worked on in some kind of way and updates will be pinned on until it is ready for the next phase.

## Plants
Still a high-level form of content, but not as general as a seedling. A draft or outlining of the seedling in a more formal way. 

A plant can branch out which is sort of a horizontal or diagonal type extension of the plant for the same idea or content in a different form or a somewhat higher level form of content, but not quite at the Budding phase yet.
