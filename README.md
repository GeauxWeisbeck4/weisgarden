# WeisGarden

My digital garden, created with Astro and made possible by the [Cactus Theme](https://github.com/chrismwilliams/astro-theme-cactus/) by [@chrismwilliams](https://github.com/chrismwilliams) - seriously, thank you for the sick setup.

A place for sharing ideas, playing with things, and creating polished pieces of content to share knowledge and important themes,  that encompass a lot of territory. I'm a very intense learner and passionate about what I do and what I believe in. My goal is to showcase these things in my writing and other things I create.

You will find everything from how to start a business, creating apps, computer science, anti-surveillance/cybersecurity knowledge, economics, psychology, philosophy, morality, politics, societal trends, corruption, democracy, techno-facism, Orwell, David Foster Wallace, Martin Luther King Jr., Zen Buddhism, mindfulness, and most important personal growth and self care.

I just overcame the most difficult period of my life which was the most important learning experience of my life. Rather then let those struggles determine the course of my life, I am going to choose to make that experience a tool for me to help others and to improve my own life.

I appreciate you checking out my project and if you want to get involved, feel free to contribute in any way. I am building a community that may be a good way to discuss some of the more important things that need to be talked about.
